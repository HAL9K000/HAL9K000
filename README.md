## About Me 🔭🌱

I am a computational ecologist, presently a PhD Candidate at the [Bonachela lab](https://www.ugr.es/~jabonachela/) at Rutgers University. I focus on the prediction and analysis of socio-ecological tipping points, such as desertification and wildfire--mediated rainforest collapse, through the lens of complex systems theory and stochastic processes.  I leverage a variety of mathematical, high--performance computational and statistical tools to do so, including mechanistic computational models, theoretical analysis, ML and computer vision techniques, remote sensing data analysis, and meta–analysis. For a more detailed overview of my research, please check out my [Research page](https://hal9k000.github.io/research.html).
